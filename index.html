What it does

Four games

Hexicon, Anagrams, Scramble and Crosswords, all in one place.

Play at your own pace

Start a game with a friend and neither of you has to be there. Take your turn whenever.

Solo too

Every game plays fine on your own if nobody is around.

A daily puzzle

A fresh board every day, the same one for everybody.

No clutter

No energy meters, no streak nagging, no ads between rounds.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, the app is free to download and play.

Do I need an account?

No. Invite a friend with a link and you are playing.

Can I play offline?

Solo games, yes. Games with a friend need a connection to sync the turns.
