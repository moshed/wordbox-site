Wordbox — Privacy

Last updated 2 September 2026

Wordbox collects as little as it can. This page says exactly what that is.

What is collected

A display name you choose and the games you play, so a game can be resumed and a friend can see whose turn it is. No email address and no password.

What is never collected

No advertising identifiers, no tracking across other apps or websites, and nothing sold or shared with data brokers. There are no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the app.

Deleting your data

Email wordbox@dancykier.com and everything tied to you is removed. Deleting the app also removes everything stored on the device.

Children

Wordbox is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.

Contact

Questions about any of this: wordbox@dancykier.com
